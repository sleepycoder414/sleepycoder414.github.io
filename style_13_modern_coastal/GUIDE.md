# Modern Coastal

Airy interiors with sand, faded blue and natural fibers, without literal nautical decoration.

**Fit for this condo: Very good.** Let the lake view supply the water reference; use sandy textiles and restrained blue.

## Main elements

- Materials: white oak; linen; woven rope; cotton; sea-glass accents.
- Shared living and dining: ivory linen sofa; round white-oak coffee table; white-oak media cabinet; oak rectangular table and six woven chairs; woven pale pendants.
- Bedrooms: oak queen beds, white cotton, faded-blue throws, abstract water art.
- Solarium: teak chairs with sea-blue cushions and teak table.
