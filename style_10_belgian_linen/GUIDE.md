# Belgian Linen Minimalism

Relaxed European restraint with generous linen, muted stone colors and worn wood.

**Fit for this condo: Very good.** Use tailored slipcovers, matte ceramics and tonal textiles against existing cream cabinetry.

## Main elements

- Materials: washed linen; weathered oak; limestone; wool; dark bronze.
- Shared living and dining: flax slipcovered sofa; round aged-oak coffee table; aged-oak media cabinet; oak rectangular dining table and six linen-seat chairs; linen drum pendants.
- Bedrooms: linen upholstered queen beds, ivory linen, mushroom throws, quiet landscape art.
- Solarium: weathered teak chairs with flax cushions and table.
