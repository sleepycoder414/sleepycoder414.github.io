# Emerald Left Bank

A jewel-green salon softened by contemporary curves and artistic restraint.

Combination: Parisian Contemporary + Contemporary Art Deco. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: emerald velvet; walnut; marble; aged brass; cream linen.
- Living and dining: emerald curved-back 205cm sofa; cream linen walnut-leg chair; oval white marble coffee table with slender brass legs; walnut media cabinet; 105cm walnut pedestal table with four cream chairs; small opal-brass pendant.
- Bedroom: emerald arched upholstered queen; walnut nightstands; ivory linen lamps; cream bedding.
- Solarium: dark teak lounge frames with emerald outdoor cushions; compact round teak dining set.

## Palette

#EEE5D6, #28564A, #986D4D, #BA9D73, #303735

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Use one stepped Deco mirror and a large black-line artwork; echo emerald through only a few smaller textiles.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid a matching jewel-tone set on every surface. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
