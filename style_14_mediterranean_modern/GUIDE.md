# Modern Mediterranean

A contemporary take on sunwashed mineral finishes, earthy ceramics and relaxed woven furniture.

**Fit for this condo: Good.** Introduce clay through cushions and pots and mineral texture through paint samples.

## Main elements

- Materials: cream plaster; terracotta; oak; linen; woven rush.
- Shared living and dining: cream linen sofa; round pale-stone coffee table; oak media cabinet; oak rectangular table and six rush-seat chairs; woven pendants.
- Bedrooms: oak queen beds, ivory linen, clay throws, earthy abstract artwork.
- Solarium: teak chairs with clay cushions and table, terracotta planters.
