# Japandi

Japanese restraint and Scandinavian comfort expressed through quiet proportions, natural materials and useful objects.

**Fit for this condo: Excellent.** Reuse the birch beds and paper lights; simplify art groupings and repeat oak, linen and olive.

## Main elements

- Materials: light oak; walnut accents; linen; rice paper; matte clay.
- Shared living and dining: oatmeal low tailored sofa; round walnut coffee table; oak media cabinet; oak rectangular dining table with six woven-seat oak chairs; white paper globe pendants.
- Bedrooms: low oak queen beds, ivory linen, muted olive throws, one ink landscape.
- Solarium: teak chairs with oatmeal cushions and teak dining table.
