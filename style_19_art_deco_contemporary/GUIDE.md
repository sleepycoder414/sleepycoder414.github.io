# Contemporary Art Deco

Geometric rhythm, rich color and refined metal details adapted to compact contemporary rooms.

**Fit for this condo: Good.** Echo the existing fluted cabinets with ribbed glass and a small number of brass accents.

## Main elements

- Materials: walnut; emerald velvet; ribbed glass; marble; brushed brass.
- Shared living and dining: emerald channel-backed sofa; round dark-marble coffee table with brass base; walnut media cabinet; walnut rectangular table and six emerald chairs; ribbed-glass pendants.
- Bedrooms: emerald upholstered queen beds, ivory bedding, bronze throws, geometric art.
- Solarium: dark teak chairs with emerald cushions and teak table.

## Implement it here

1. Begin with a textile and paint sample set beside the existing floor, cream cabinet front and black marble. Review by the windows and farther inside under evening light.
2. Echo the existing fluted cabinets with ribbed glass and a small number of brass accents.
3. Set the shared living/dining palette first. Keep sofa, TV, coffee table and dining placement consistent with the reference renders; use the specified furniture family across all three views.
4. Carry the bedroom fabric and wood family into both bedrooms. Retain each room's door/window configuration and queen bed footprint; keep the north bedroom gallery distinct from the primary's single artwork.
5. Repeat the outdoor chair/cushion family in both solarium zones. Keep its dark tile, wood ceiling, end walls, glazing and structural pillar. Choose fabrics and furniture suited to actual solar exposure and moisture conditions.
6. Use matching lamp finishes and warm diffused light. Keep hanging fittings proportionate to the 2.700 m ceiling and clear of doors and circulation.
7. Buy samples and one anchor textile before replacing large pieces; then resolve rugs, lamps and art. Recheck physical dimensions in the Blender model before purchasing furniture.

## Watch-outs

Use geometry in textiles rather than adding bulky decorative construction. Existing IKEA pieces may be retained where compatible; generated alternatives are design concepts, not identifiable purchasable products. Generic examples may show different architecture. Condo edits must preserve the existing shell; any image-generation drift is not an approved construction change.
