## Original collection: styles 01–20

The main gallery and every style page offer **Save to shortlist** and **Not for me**. Marking a style “Not for me” moves it to a separate section at the bottom of the gallery. **Restore to main list** brings it back; saving it to the shortlist also restores it. Excluding a shortlisted style removes it from the shortlist. Choices persist in your browser, with search and collection filters available for both sections.

Curated 2026 shortlist of 20 current and enduring styles, ordered for comparison and condo fit, not a measured global popularity ranking. Created 2026-09-12. The style combinations are editorial interpretations, not formal categories with fixed boundaries.

Start with Pacific Northwest Modern, Japandi, Warm Scandinavian and Organic Modern. These work naturally with the lake outlook, existing pale wood, cream kitchen and black solarium framing. For richer color compare Mid-century Modern, Contemporary Art Deco and Curated Maximalism.

The current empty-shell revision contains 10 selected images per style: a material moodboard, a color palette, three generic examples (living, bedroom, dining), and five furnished edits of the current condo references: 200 images total. ## Research basis

The 2026 1stDibs designer survey highlights maximalism, eclecticism, organic modernism, chocolate brown and vintage sourcing. This supports including both expressive and restrained options; it does not rank all twenty styles here. [1stDibs 2026 survey](https://investors.1stdibs.com/news/news-details/2025/2026-Interior-Design-Trends-1stDibs-Survey-Identifies-Maximalism-Chocolate-Brown-and-Vintage-Antiques-as-Top-Designer-Choices/default.aspx).

Houzz's 2026 reporting describes continued interest in warmth, natural materials, texture and traditional detail. These broad signals inform this shortlist. [Houzz 2026 trends](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/25-home-design-trends-defining-how-well-live-in-2026-stsetivw-vs~184059289).

All room prescriptions and palettes below are original design recommendations for this project. Hex values are digital concept colors, not paint-product matches; inspect physical samples in the condo's daylight before specifying finishes.
