# Warm Scandinavian

Light, practical Nordic rooms softened by tactile textiles and warm wood rather than stark white.

**Fit for this condo: Excellent.** Keep most current furniture; add washable wool-look textiles, light blue accents and diffused lamps.

## Main elements

- Materials: pale ash; birch; wool; cotton; opal glass.
- Shared living and dining: pale sage tailored sofa; round pale-oak coffee table; pale-oak media cabinet; pale-ash rectangular dining table and six spindle chairs; opal globe pendants.
- Bedrooms: birch spindle beds, white cotton, blue-gray throws, simple botanical prints.
- Solarium: light teak chairs with sage cushions and matching table.

3. Set the shared living/dining palette first. Keep sofa, TV, coffee table and dining placement consistent with the reference renders; use the specified furniture family across all three views.
4. Carry the bedroom fabric and wood family into both bedrooms. Retain each room's door/window configuration and queen bed footprint; keep the north bedroom gallery distinct from the primary's single artwork.
5. Repeat the outdoor chair/cushion family in both solarium zones. Keep its dark tile, wood ceiling, end walls, glazing and structural pillar. Choose fabrics and furniture suited to actual solar exposure and moisture conditions.
