# Parisian Contemporary

An edited mix of graceful vintage pieces, contemporary upholstery and artistic detail.

**Fit for this condo: Good.** Use one antique-style mirror or lamp and expressive art rather than recreating a period apartment.

## Main elements

- Materials: walnut; ivory linen; marble; brass; muted velvet.
- Shared living and dining: ivory curved-arm sofa; round marble coffee table; walnut media cabinet; walnut rectangular table and six taupe upholstered chairs; brass and opal pendants.
- Bedrooms: ivory upholstered queen beds, white linen, dusty-mauve throws, abstract black-line art.
- Solarium: dark teak chairs with ivory cushions and teak table.
