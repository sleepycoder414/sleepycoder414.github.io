# Burgundy & Brass Gallery

Burgundy, warm ivory and sculptural brass make a lively but composed gallery-like home.

Combination: Parisian Contemporary + Contemporary Art Deco + Quiet Luxury Contemporary. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: burgundy velvet; ivory linen; walnut; dark marble; brushed brass.
- Living and dining: burgundy low curved 205cm sofa; ivory linen club chair; oval dark-marble coffee table on slim brass legs; walnut media cabinet; 105cm walnut pedestal table and four ivory chairs; brass globe pendant.
- Bedroom: burgundy arched queen headboard; walnut nightstands; linen brass lamps; ivory bedding.
- Solarium: dark teak frames with burgundy outdoor cushions; round teak dining set.

## Palette

#EDE4D4, #713D47, #936B4C, #B49A6B, #2B3334

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Make one colorful abstract artwork the focal point, with a restrained brass mirror and a quiet wool rug.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid ornate gold trim and wall-to-wall dark finishes. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
