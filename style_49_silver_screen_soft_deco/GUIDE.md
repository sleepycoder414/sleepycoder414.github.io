# Silver Screen Soft Deco

Dove-gray curves and reflective accents evoke old cinema in a light residential room.

Combination: Quiet Luxury Contemporary + Parisian Contemporary + Contemporary Art Deco. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: dove velvet; ivory boucle; silvered mirror; dark walnut; nickel.
- Living and dining: dove-gray curved 205cm sofa; ivory boucle swivel chair; round pale-stone coffee table with nickel base; dark walnut media cabinet; 105cm round dark walnut table and four dove chairs; opal nickel pendant.
- Bedroom: dove fan-shaped queen headboard; dark walnut nightstands; opal lamps; ivory quilt.
- Solarium: charcoal aluminum lounge frames with gray cushions; round outdoor dining.

## Palette

#EDE9E1, #A8AAAD, #C7BDB1, #62504A, #323A41

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Use a single stepped silver mirror and a low-contrast scallop rug; keep reflections localized.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid mirror-clad furniture and theatrical ceiling treatments. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
