A piano lounge for everyday life

Kawai NV12: 151.5 cm wide × 87 cm deep. Reserve additional bench and playing space, plus a separate circulation route. Pair a 180 cm sofa and comfortable chair with a 100 cm round dining table for four. The piano stays on the solid living-room wall, clear of the refrigerator; TV beside it, never above the opening lid.

Use dimmable table and floor lamps, one textured feature wall, a small stepped Art Deco mirror and two framed jazz portraits. Choose ready-made furniture and veneer, then spend selectively on comfortable upholstery and lighting. The full guide explains daytime living, evening lighting and furnishing priorities.

Build the room in layers

  1. Start with upholstery and timber samples beside the existing floor, cream cabinetry and black marble.
  2. Use warm oatmeal linen-look wallpaper at the piano, plum and rose in upholstery, brass light pools, and a small joyful geometric print rather than an expensive feature wall.
  3. Resolve the sofa, chair, media cabinet and dining set together. Repeat the selected designs across the shared room.
  4. Carry the material family into the bedroom and outdoor furnishings, then finish with rugs, lamps and artwork.
  5. Check actual dimensions and chair pull-out space in the calibrated Blender model before purchasing.

Room essentials

Queen mattress: 152.4 × 203.2 cm. Ceiling: 2.7 m. Compact dining target: approximately 105 cm round or 120 × 75 cm rectangular, seating four.
