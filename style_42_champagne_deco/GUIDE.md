# Champagne Deco

Soft champagne tones give Deco geometry a calm everyday character.

Combination: Quiet Luxury Contemporary + Contemporary Art Deco. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: champagne velvet; pale walnut; ribbed glass; cream stone; satin brass.
- Living and dining: champagne channel-back 205cm sofa; ivory tub chair; round cream-stone coffee table with thin brass ring; pale walnut fluted media cabinet; 105cm round pale walnut dining table and four champagne chairs; ribbed-glass pendant.
- Bedroom: champagne channel headboard queen; pale walnut nightstands; ribbed glass table lamps; ivory bedding.
- Solarium: teak and sand rope chairs; champagne outdoor cushions; round teak dining table.

## Palette

#F0E7D6, #CFBA91, #9E805B, #B4AAA0, #333534

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Concentrate fluting on one cabinet and use a subtle cream fan-pattern rug; a small stepped mirror supplies the Deco accent.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid shiny gold on every furniture edge. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
