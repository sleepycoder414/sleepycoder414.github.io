# Sapphire Geometry

Sapphire upholstery and precise geometry create a cool, polished room with warm edges.

Combination: Contemporary Art Deco + Quiet Luxury Contemporary. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: sapphire velvet; gray wool; walnut; smoked glass; brushed brass.
- Living and dining: sapphire channel 210cm sofa; warm-gray wool chair; oval smoked-glass coffee table on brass legs; walnut media cabinet; 105cm walnut round dining table and four blue-gray chairs; ribbed-glass pendant.
- Bedroom: sapphire channel queen headboard; walnut nightstands; brass opal lamps; gray-blue throw.
- Solarium: dark teak frames and blue outdoor cushions; teak dining set.

## Palette

#EEE7DB, #294B68, #8E999C, #765E4D, #BEA675

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Repeat a restrained stepped motif in the rug and mirror, keeping most walls ivory.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid blue accent lighting and glossy blue cabinetry. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
