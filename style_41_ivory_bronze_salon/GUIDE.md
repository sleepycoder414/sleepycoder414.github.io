# Ivory & Bronze Salon

Ivory upholstery and graceful walnut shapes with a single vintage mirror.

Combination: Quiet Luxury Contemporary + Parisian Contemporary. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: ivory wool; walnut; honed cream stone; brushed bronze; taupe velvet.
- Living and dining: ivory slim curved-arm 210cm sofa; taupe velvet barrel chair; oval cream-stone coffee table on walnut legs; slim walnut media cabinet; 105cm round walnut dining table with four ivory upholstered chairs; twin bronze opal pendant.
- Bedroom: ivory curved upholstered queen; walnut nightstands; bronze dome lamps; taupe throw.
- Solarium: dark teak and taupe outdoor seating; round teak dining set.

## Palette

#EEE7DA, #B8A791, #795D49, #8B7771, #353434

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Choose one bronze-framed vintage mirror and quiet landscape art; keep the rest of the room tailored.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid ornate moldings and oversized chandeliers. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
