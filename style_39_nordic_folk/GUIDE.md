# Nordic Folk Modern

Simple pale timber furniture animated by woven pattern and handmade color.

**Design combination:** Scandinavian + folk craft. This is an editorial furnishing proposal, not a statistical popularity ranking.

## Main elements

- Materials: pine; woven wool; indigo cotton; red painted wood; glazed ceramic.
- Living and dining: cream pine-leg sofa; indigo woven pine armchair; red painted oval coffee table; pine media cabinet; compact pine table with four spindle chairs; pleated paper pendant; restrained red-blue folk-pattern rug.
- Primary bedroom: pine spindle queen; ivory quilt and indigo-red woven throw; pine nightstands and blue ceramic lamps.
- Solarium: teak outdoor seating with indigo performance cushions and red patterned pillows; simple teak dining set.

## Palette

#ECE3D0, #405E79, #AD4D3E, #CBA956, #A9B296

Use the first tone as a quiet base, repeat timber/material colors across the room, and distribute accent colors through upholstery, artwork and lamps. Digital colors are references rather than exact paint matches; compare physical samples with the cream cabinetry, existing floor and black marble.

## Implement it in this condo

1. Put the strongest woven pattern on one rug and keep upholstery mostly solid.
2. Begin with the sofa, accent chair, coffee table and media cabinet as one coordinated furniture family. Keep the TV on the solid wall and the sofa near the glazing. Use the same pieces in all three shared-room views.
3. Choose a compact dining table for exactly four people, approximately 105 cm diameter or 120 x 75 cm rectangular. Check chair pull-out space and the kitchen approach in the measured model.
4. Use a queen mattress (152.4 x 203.2 cm), two compact nightstands and two lamps. Keep sliding doors, the operable window bay and bedroom access usable.
5. Carry the palette outdoors through weatherproof cushions and wood, rope or powder-coated metal frames. Keep the solarium pillar and passageways clear. The left glass in the solarium view looks into the living room.
6. Add one coherent rug and art scheme, then coordinate lamp finishes. Keep pendants proportionate to the 2.7 m ceiling.
7. Test material samples in daylight and evening light. Resolve actual furniture dimensions in Blender before purchase; the AI images are furnishing concepts with possible small differences between views.

## What to avoid

Avoid costume-like themed objects or mixing unrelated motifs.
