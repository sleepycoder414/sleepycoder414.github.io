# Organic Modern

Modern simplicity with rounded silhouettes and the texture of stone, wood and natural cloth.

**Fit for this condo: Excellent.** Introduce one rounded upholstered anchor and a tactile rug while retaining the cream kitchen.

## Main elements

- Materials: oak; travertine; boucle; linen; matte plaster.
- Shared living and dining: cream softly rounded sofa; round travertine coffee table; oak media cabinet; oak rectangular dining table with six curved oak chairs; cream sculptural pendants.
- Bedrooms: cream upholstered queen beds, sand linen, olive throws, textured abstract art.
- Solarium: teak chairs with cream cushions and teak table.
