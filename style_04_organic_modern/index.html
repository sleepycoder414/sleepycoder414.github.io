05 / Make it yours

From a feeling to a furnishing plan.

Living & dining

cream softly rounded sofa; round travertine coffee table; oak media cabinet; oak rectangular dining table with four curved oak chairs; cream sculptural pendants.

One sofa, one accent chair, coffee table, TV and media cabinet. A compact indoor dining table for four.

Primary bedroom

cream upholstered queen beds, sand linen, olive throws, textured abstract art.

Queen bed, two compact nightstands and two lamps. Preserve access to doors and operable glazing.

Solarium

teak chairs with cream cushions and teak table.

Use outdoor frames and weatherproof textiles; keep the structural pillar and passages clear.

Build the room in layers

  1. Start with upholstery and timber samples beside the existing floor, cream cabinetry and black marble.
  2. Introduce one rounded upholstered anchor and a tactile rug while retaining the cream kitchen.
  3. Resolve the sofa, chair, media cabinet and dining set together. Repeat the selected designs across the shared room.
  4. Carry the material family into the bedroom and outdoor furnishings, then finish with rugs, lamps and artwork.
  5. Check actual dimensions and chair pull-out space in the calibrated Blender model before purchasing.

Room essentials

Queen mattress: 152.4 × 203.2 cm. Ceiling: 2.7 m. Compact dining target: approximately 105 cm round or 120 × 75 cm rectangular, seating four.
