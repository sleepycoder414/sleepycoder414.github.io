# Olive Atelier

An artful olive and parchment room with comfortable shapes and an unfussy collected feel.

Combination: Parisian Contemporary + Quiet Luxury Contemporary. A variation on the user's three favorite styles, differentiated by furniture shape, color, texture and art.

## Main elements

- Materials: olive linen; parchment wool; walnut; travertine; bronze.
- Living and dining: olive tailored curved-arm 205cm sofa; parchment wool chair; oval travertine coffee table; walnut media cabinet; 105cm walnut table and four parchment chairs; bronze linen pendant.
- Bedroom: olive low upholstered queen; walnut nightstands; parchment ceramic lamps; cream bedding.
- Solarium: teak and olive outdoor cushions; compact teak dining set.

## Palette

#EAE5D8, #72775B, #B4A384, #795E47, #353C35

Keep the existing pale floor, cream kitchen and black marble as the sample backdrop. Physical materials need checking in daylight and warm evening light. These digital swatches are design references, not paint specifications.

## Implementation

1. Layer two differently sized artworks beside a small vintage mirror; use olive linen rather than high-shine velvet.
2. Begin with upholstery and timber samples. Repeat the selected sofa, chair, tables, lighting and art across all three living/dining views.
3. Maintain a separate everyday dining table for four. Choose compact chairs that tuck in; keep the route to the kitchen and bedroom doors open.
4. Use a queen mattress 152.4 x 203.2 cm and two compact nightstands with two lamps. Carry the material family into bedding and one artwork.
5. Choose outdoor-rated frames and cushions for the solarium; keep its black tile, wood ceiling, glazing and black structural pillar. Use plants and movable lamps for atmosphere.
6. Layer warm lamps with a usable task light over the dining table. Buy lamps and sample textiles first, then larger furniture. Avoid purchasing an entire matching suite.

## Watch-outs

Avoid rustic distressing and too many small objects. Preserve all existing openings, floors, kitchen geometry and 2.7 m ceiling. Renders are furnishing concepts; small image-generation variations are not approved construction changes. Verify furniture and circulation in the measured model before purchase.
