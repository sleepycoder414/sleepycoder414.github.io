## Three residential Jazz Piano Lounges

- [51 Emerald Encore](style_51_jazz_emerald_encore/index.html): emerald velvet, cognac, amber light and classic jazz portraits.
- [52 Blue Hour](style_52_jazz_blue_hour/index.html): ink blue, oatmeal, smoked oak and quiet evening atmosphere.
- [53 Plum Rhythm](style_53_jazz_plum_rhythm/index.html): plum, dusty rose, mustard accents and playful geometric art.

Each balances a compact sofa and comfortable chair with a four-seat everyday dining table, TV and the Kawai NV12. The guides explain affordable layers: plug-in lamps, dimmable warm bulbs, limited wallpaper, framed prints and a modest Deco mirror. The piano reference has a shortened grand-style body; the NV12 measures 151.5 × 87 × 102.5 cm closed, and reaches 172.5 cm with its lid fully open. See [Kawai specifications](https://www.kawai-global.com/product/nv12/) and the supplied references in `ref/`.

The images are furnishing concepts. They preserve the main room arrangement while allowing minor differences in artwork, lighting, upholstery details and apparent scale between views. Piano bench space, lid clearance, circulation and actual product dimensions require measured placement in the current Blender model before purchasing. No model geometry was changed.
